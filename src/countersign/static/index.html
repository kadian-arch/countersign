Built to be trusted

How your money stays safe

The assistant reads email from people you have never met. These are the rules the guard applies to everything it tries to do.

Six checks on every refund and payment

  • IdentityThe mail server's SPF, DKIM and DMARC result decides who really sent a message. Only a confirmed message from the owner's address is the owner. Anything that fails is held.
  • AuthorityA customer can only ask for a refund on their own order, and a supplier's invoice is paid only on the word of a contact on record for that supplier.
  • Need to knowLookups return nothing about orders, payments or invoices that do not belong to the sender, so the assistant cannot repeat what it never saw.
  • ProvenanceEvery amount and payee is traced to where it came from. A figure that appears only in text a stranger wrote does not move money.
  • Limits and approvalThe owner sets the limits. Anything above them waits for approval, and approval re-checks the guard before anything is sent.
  • AuditEvery attempt is written to a hash-chained log, and each reply is checked against it before it is sent.
